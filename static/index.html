How it works

1. Gemini reads the document (PDF or a phone photo) and returns the vendor, dates, PO number, every line item and the totals, exactly as printed.

2. Plain Python rules check it: line maths, subtotal, total, dates, duplicates, and that the key numbers really appear in the PDF text (catches made-up values).

3. It is matched to a purchase order: vendor by tax ID or fuzzy name ("Harbour Office Supplies" → Harbor Office Supply LLC), PO by number even when written "P.O. #1046", then each line for quantity left and price (2% tolerance).

4. Clean invoices are approved automatically. Anything else waits here with the reasons. Fix a field and it is re-checked; approve or reject with a note. Approved invoices go to CSV or a webhook (n8n, Zapier, your ERP).
